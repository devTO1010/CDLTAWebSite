Class A full course

200 hours. 20 weekdays.

The Class A course prepares students for safe, compliant operation of combination vehicles. It includes FMCSA ELDT theory, classroom, range, and public-road driving.

Tuition

CDL full tuition is $5,350. Administrative fee $150, nonrefundable. Registration $100. Materials $225. Optional Road Ready English $1,000. Optional retest prep $120 a session. DOT physical $110 and drug test $65 are paid to the provider.

The old site listed $6,300. That figure is retired.

To enroll

  • At least 18, or 21 for interstate driving.
  • Legal residency or citizenship, and a valid driver license.
  • DOT medical exam and drug test, paid to the provider.
  • English sufficient for safety, instruction, and testing. ESL support is available.

To graduate

  • Complete at least 90 percent of program hours.
  • Pass ELDT theory and the required skill evaluations.
  • Meet attendance and conduct rules, and satisfy tuition.
