About

A Shepherdsville school for Class A drivers.

CDL Trucking Academy trains people in the Louisville area to operate a Class A combination vehicle and to handle the English used on the job. Training is the product. There is no job placement promise and no income guarantee.

Safety first

Pre-trip, air brakes, hours of service, and defensive driving.

Clear English

Inspections, paperwork, and talking with officers and customers.

Straight pricing

Tuition is $5,350. Optional English is separate.
