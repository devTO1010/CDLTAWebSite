A Shepherdsville school for Class A drivers.

The school trains people to operate a Class A combination vehicle and to handle the English used in the cab and at the scale. Training is the product. There is no placement offer and no income promise. All training is in person at 368 Ferguson Ln, Shepherdsville, KY 40165, up to 2 students per class with one instructor. Licensure application pending with KCPE; the school will be registered on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry after licensure.
