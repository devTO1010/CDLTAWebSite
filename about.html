About

A Shepherdsville school for Class A drivers

CDL Trucking Academy is a commercial driver school in Shepherdsville, Kentucky. The purpose is to help people in the Louisville area earn a Class A CDL and start a trucking career, with extra support for English learners.

What we stand for

  • Safety and compliance first.
  • Clear trucking English for inspections, paperwork, and the radio.
  • Hands-on yard and road coaching.
  • Transparent pricing. No job or income guarantee.
