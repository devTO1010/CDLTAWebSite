Why CDL Trucking Academy

Safety first

Pre-trip, air brakes, hours of service, and defensive driving taught to FMCSA Entry-Level Driver Training standards.

Hands-on

Classroom, range maneuvers, and behind-the-wheel road time. Small cohorts and mock tests.

Clear English

Optional Road Ready English for inspections, bills of lading, and talking with officers, employers, and customers.

The journey

1. Enroll

Valid license, DOT physical, drug screen, and the enrollment agreement. 18 to test in Kentucky, 21 for interstate.

2. Train

About 200 clock hours over 20 weekdays: theory, classroom, range, road, and testing practice.

3. Test

Kentucky State Police knowledge and skills tests. The school does not issue the license.
